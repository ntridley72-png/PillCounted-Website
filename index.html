Questions

Good to know

Is Pillcounted a replacement for my pharmacist?

No. Pillcounted is a self-tracking and information aid. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, and a photograph alone cannot confirm a pill. Always confirm what you're taking with a pharmacist or prescriber.

Can it tell me my exact copay?

It points you to your plan's official pricing tool and shows discount-card and coupon options. The exact copay depends on your deductible, formulary tier, pharmacy and quantity, so only your insurer or pharmacy can confirm it.

Where is my information kept?

Your medications, dose logs and family profiles stay on your device, encrypted at rest and locked behind Face ID. Features that need an outside service — like an FDA lookup — send only the minimum required, and say so in the app.

Does it store or sell my health data?

No. Medication and prescription details are never used for advertising or analytics, and your health information is never sold. You can delete your account and associated data from inside the app.
